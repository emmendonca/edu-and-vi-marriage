What to do in Lyon?

From around 1pm or 2pm till the starting of the party there are no specific activities planned. So feel free to explore the surroundings and enjoy the atmosphere! Next, are some suggestions:

Vieux Lyon and Fourvière Hill

Put on some comfortable shoes and get lost in old streets of Vieux Lyon. Then at the hidden panoramic lookout at Jardin des Curiosités. Discover the history of the ancient Théâtre Romain before making your way to the top at Fourvière for an incredible city view!

Tip: there is a funicular if you want to skip the climb :D!

TCL Boat to Confluence & Museum

Hop aboard the TCL Vaporetto boat for a relaxing ride down the river into Lyon's sleek modern district, Confluence. Drop by the impressive Musée des Confluences to check out its futuristic architecture right at the point where the two rivers meet!

Tip: there is an stop for the vaporeto very close to the apero place.

Lyon Contemporary Art Biennale

Dive into contemporary art with tours and workshops at the Biennale de Lyon! Explore installations inside the former railway yards at Les Grandes Locos and check out cutting-edge exhibitions over at macLYON.

Musée des Beaux-Arts de Lyon

Explore centuries of art at the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Lyon, housed in a stunning 17th-century Benedictine abbey. The museum is in the lively Place des Terreaux, heart of the city! Make sure to take a quiet break in the museum's peaceful sculpture garden at Palais Saint-Pierre.

Walk along the Rhône & Saône Rivers

If it's sunny, take a relaxing walk or ride a bike along the water! Check out the chill grass spots and riverboat cafes along the Berges du Rhône, or head over to the Rives de Saône for amazing views of Old Lyon and the famous Fourvière Church.

Explore Île Barbe

Before joining us for drinks, take a quick walk around Île Barbe! This quiet little island right in the middle of the Saône River is packed with history, ancient stone buildings, and the old Eglise Notre-Dame. It’s right across from our party venue, making it the perfect spot for a peaceful stroll right before the party starts!
